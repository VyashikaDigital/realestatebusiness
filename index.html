Buying and renting guides

How much house can you afford?

Start with your monthly budget, not the lender's maximum. Keep housing near 28 percent of gross income.

Renting or buying: how to decide

If you plan to stay five years or more and can cover repairs, buying usually wins. Otherwise renting keeps you flexible.

Five checks before making an offer

Inspection, comparable sales, HOA fees, property taxes and the roof's age.
