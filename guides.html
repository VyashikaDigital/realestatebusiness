Buying and renting guides

How much house can you afford?

Start with your monthly comfort level, not the maximum a lender approves. A common guideline keeps housing near 28 percent of gross income and total debt under 36 percent.

Add the costs people forget: property tax, insurance, HOA dues, maintenance (budget 1 percent of the price per year) and utilities.

Renting or buying: how to decide

Buying tends to win when you plan to stay five years or more, have an emergency fund after the down payment, and can cover repairs.

Renting wins when your plans may change, you want lower upfront costs, or local prices are high compared with rents.

Five checks before you make an offer

Review recent comparable sales so your offer matches the market. Ask for a professional inspection and read it carefully.

Confirm HOA fees, property tax history and the age of the roof, HVAC and water heater. Know your financing: a pre-approval letter makes your offer stronger.
